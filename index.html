What Drives Us
We build games you can feel. Tactile board games with the weight of carved wooden pieces. Spatial puzzles where every move clicks into place. Smart tools that solve real problems in one tap.

Trigemeo is a small indie studio with a simple philosophy: no lootbox traps, no data harvesting, no bloatware. Just honest, polished digital craft — made by people who actually play their own games.
We brew strong coffee. We break physics engines. We ship.
