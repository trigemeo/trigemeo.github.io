Purchases & Subscriptions

Open the app and look for a "Restore Purchases" button in the settings or store section. Your purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored on any device.

Subscriptions are managed through your device:

  • Apple: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.
  • Google: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions.
⚠️ Uninstalling the app does NOT cancel your subscription. Please cancel before uninstalling.

First, try the "Restore Purchases" function. If the issue persists, contact us at support@trigemeo.com with your purchase receipt. You can also request a refund directly from Apple or Google.

Your Rights

Account & Data Deletion

You are always in control of your data. Here's how to delete it.

1

Delete Local Game Data

All game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Action: Uninstall the app. This permanently removes all local data.

2

Manage Platform Data (Game Center / Google Play Games)

If you used Apple Game Center or Google Play Games for multiplayer, leaderboards, or achievements:

  • Apple: Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Game Center to manage your data.
  • Google: Go to play.google.com/settings or Settings → Google → Play Games.
3

Cancel Subscriptions (If Applicable)

If you have an active premium subscription, cancel it before uninstalling:

  • Apple: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → [App Name] → Cancel.
  • Google: Google Play → Menu → Subscriptions → [App Name] → Cancel.
⚠️ Uninstalling the app does NOT cancel active subscriptions.
4

Request Full Data Erasure

If you want to request a complete deletion of any data we may hold:

  • Send an email to support@trigemeo.com with the subject: "Data Deletion Request".
  • Include the app name and your device platform (iOS / Android).
  • We will confirm the deletion within 48 hours.

For privacy-related requests, you may also contact privacy@trigemeo.com.
